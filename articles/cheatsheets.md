## Illustrated guide

Five posters drawn by Larissa S. M. Sugai, which go through the stages
in detail: validation, the diagnostic, retraining, calibration, and the
community matrices with occupancy. They refer to each other as Figures 1
to 5, the order of the pages.

[Download the
PDF](https://provetelab.org/sheepdog/cheatsheets/sheepdog-illustrated-guide.pdf)
(five pages).

The posters predate the review rounds. For another round, use
`select_clips(previous = )` and
[`combine_rounds()`](https://provetelab.org/sheepdog/reference/combine_rounds.md)
rather than a new seed
([`vignette("validation-effort")`](https://provetelab.org/sheepdog/articles/validation-effort.md)).
“Routes to detection histories and occupancy” is now the article
[Choosing a
route](https://provetelab.org/sheepdog/articles/choosing-a-route.md).
